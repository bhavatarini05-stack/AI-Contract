STANDARD SERVICE AGREEMENT (Company template)

This Service Agreement is entered into as of [DATE] by and between [PROVIDER NAME] ("Provider") and [CLIENT NAME] ("Client").

1. Services
Provider shall provide the services described in the applicable Statement of Work in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards.

2. Term
The initial term of this Agreement shall be twelve (12) months commencing on the Effective Date.

3. Renewal
This Agreement may be renewed for additional one (1) year periods by mutual written agreement of the parties. Either party may give written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

4. Fees and Payment
Client shall pay the fees set out in the Statement of Work. Client shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt. Late payments shall accrue interest at 1% per month.

5. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other party and use it only to perform this Agreement. These obligations survive for three (3) years after termination. Upon termination, each party shall return or destroy the other party's Confidential Information.

6. Indemnification
Each party shall indemnify and defend the other party against third-party claims to the extent caused by the indemnifying party's breach of this Agreement, gross negligence or wilful misconduct.

7. Limitation of Liability
Except for breaches of confidentiality and indemnification obligations, each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid by Client in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim. In no event shall either party be liable for any indirect, incidental, special or consequential damages, including lost profits.

8. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within such period. Client may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) days' written notice.

9. Intellectual Property
Client shall own all deliverables created specifically for Client under this Agreement. Provider retains all rights in its pre-existing intellectual property and grants Client a non-exclusive licence to use it as part of the deliverables.

10. Data Protection
Each party shall comply with applicable data protection laws, including the GDPR where applicable. Provider shall maintain appropriate technical and organisational security measures, shall notify Client of any personal data breach without undue delay and in any event within 72 hours, and shall not engage sub-processors without Client's prior written consent. On termination Provider shall return or delete all personal data.

11. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York. Disputes shall first be escalated to senior management and, failing resolution within thirty (30) days, submitted to the courts of New York County.

12. General
Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both parties.
